API design (JS / TS) — Discord bots, REST APIs, and website backends — and I also dabble
in AI/NLP training and embedded hardware on the side, though backend is where I spend most
of my time and am most comfortable. Most of what I build stays private or proprietary; this
account mainly holds projects I've open-sourced, or archived to keep a record.
